Dataset: NSL-KDD
Source: Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity (CIC), University of New Brunswick.
  https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/nsl.html

NSL-KDD is a revised version of the KDD Cup 1999 dataset. Please cite:

  M. Tavallaee, E. Bagheri, W. Lu, and A. A. Ghorbani, "A Detailed Analysis of
  the KDD CUP 99 Data Set," Second IEEE Symposium on Computational Intelligence
  for Security and Defense Applications (CISDA), 2009.

The raw files are fetched at build time by ../../tools/prepare_nsl_kdd.py from a
public mirror; they are not redistributed in this repository. Verify the CIC
terms of use before redistributing derived splits.

The per-family splits, binary label mapping, metric definition, and SOTA/baseline
anchors in this package are original to OpenRSI and are provided for research use
under the repository's license (CC BY-NC 4.0).
